03 / WHAT YOU CAN TEST

Inspect the proposal.

Compare turned and near-frontal heads, independent eye gaze, hair over a cheek, and the face/background composition. The angle controls are selections between keys: changing a pose while retaining a key would change its frozen portrait.

For test keys 1 and 2, choose a separate saved CPU render of the same key. “Check every pixel” verifies the PNG and raw RGBA checksums and compares all 1,048,576 channel bytes, including the background. It reads the original PNG scanlines rather than using browser display pixels.

To generate a fresh public key and independently regenerate its illustrated portrait in your browser, use the live key generator.

RESEARCH STATUS

More realism to build.

Skin detail, age and facial variety, natural hair, eye anatomy, clothing and landscape quality need further work. These examples establish exact-repeat evidence on the pinned CPU reference. They do not establish conformance on other render machines.

A private intersection can be remembered by the person. This prototype does not measure how much that obstructs an attacker’s search for a different valid key. No checkpoint entry, click marker, analytics or view-position storage is included.
