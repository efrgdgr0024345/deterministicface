DeterministicFace research demo

The current TypeScript procedural geometry and software rasterizer were written for this project.
No third-party face images, fonts or trained model weights are included in this candidate.
Native Web Crypto implements SHA-256, HKDF and Ed25519. TypeScript is development tooling by Microsoft, licensed Apache-2.0.

Foundational research and implementation precedents (not claims of replication):
Adrian Perrig and Dawn Song — Hash Visualization (1999).
Their credited Random Art lineage: Andrej Bauer; Michael Witbrock and John Mount.
Mozhgan Azimpourkivi, Umut Topkara and Bogdan Carbunar — CEAL (USENIX Security 2020).
Alexander Becker — FaceHash (hash-to-StyleGAN face implementation).
Tero Karras, Samuli Laine, Miika Aittala, Janne Hellsten, Jaakko Lehtinen and Timo Aila — StyleGAN2.
Joshua Tan, Lujo Bauer, Joseph Bonneau, Lorrie Faith Cranor, Jeremy Thomas and Blase Ur — Can Unicorns Help Users Compare Crypto Key Fingerprints? (2017).

This demo does not incorporate FaceHash or StyleGAN model code/weights; their comparison remains future work.
